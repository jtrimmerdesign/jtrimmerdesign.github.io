I worked across GTA Online from The Contract in 2021 through to The Chop Shop in 2023, contributing to five major updates across that period.

Content was authored using a combination of a visual content creation tool and structured data scripting, a hybrid approach that allowed designers to shape mission flow, pacing and escalation without writing bespoke code. The design challenge throughout was keeping missions fresh in a game where players had seen everything and expectations were high.

As with RDO, the team developed a templating approach to deliver content at scale across multiple updates, allowing mission logic and structure to be established once and rapidly adapted across different locations and scenarios.
