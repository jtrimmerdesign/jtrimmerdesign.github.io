Red Dead Online

Role

Release Date

www.rockstargames.com/reddeadonline/

I worked across Red Dead Online from beta through to Blood Money in 2021, contributing to every major role release during that period including Bounty Hunter, Trader, Collector, Moonshiner and Naturalist.

Content was authored using a combination of a visual content creation tool and structured data scripting, a hybrid approach that allowed designers to shape mission flow, pacing and escalation without writing bespoke code. The design challenge throughout was making a fundamentally repeatable structure (go somewhere, get something, bring it back) feel varied, purposeful and worth repeating across hundreds of pieces of content.
