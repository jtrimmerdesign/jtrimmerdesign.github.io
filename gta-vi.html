I worked on GTA VI at Rockstar North, designing and implementing content using more complex design and implementation methods, including in-engine visual scripting and C#. That meant adapting quickly to new proprietary tooling and learning C# scripting on the job, and extending and adapting existing gameplay systems to support specific design goals within technical and production constraints.

As one of the earlier members of my team to onboard onto the project, I spent time establishing what worked and what didn't, then passing that knowledge on to colleagues joining later to help speed up their onboarding.
